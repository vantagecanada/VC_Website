About Us

Vantage Canada is a media brand dedicated to inspiring ambition, sharing the stories of our great Canadians, and challenging Canadians to build a stronger, more prosperous nation.

Our Vision

To build a world-leading Canada where ambition, innovation, and excellence drive our nation's future.

Our Mission

To tell compelling Canadian stories, highlight exceptional people, and inspire Canadians to help shape a stronger and more prosperous future.

Our Values

Ambition, integrity, excellence, and a commitment to Canada's future. We believe in the potential of Canadians and the power of ideas to build a stronger nation.
